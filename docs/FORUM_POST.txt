Hi,

I made a small desktop widget for Windows that shows battery level (and charging status) for your Razer wireless gear in one place: headphones, keyboard, and mouse. It uses the same idea as razer-taskbar (reads Synapse logs), so you need Razer Synapse 3 or 4 running—no extra drivers.

• Download (installer): https://github.com/LeonardoVidolin/razer-battery-widget/releases/latest
• Source & readme: https://github.com/LeonardoVidolin/razer-battery-widget

It sits on the desktop with a Razer-style dark/green look, minimizes to the tray, and you can enable “Start with Windows” from the tray menu. Works on Windows 10 and 11.

If you try it and something doesn’t work or a device isn’t detected, say which one and I can try to add support.
