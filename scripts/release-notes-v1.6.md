### Added
- **Change a device's icon**: right-click a device in the widget and pick **Show as** Headphones, Keyboard, Mouse, Speaker, Controller or Other device. Auto-detect puts it back. The choice is remembered for each device.
- **Hide a single device**: right-click a device and choose **Hide this device**. This helps with things like a wired keyboard that Synapse always reports at 100%. To bring a device back, right-click the widget background and use **Hidden devices**.
- **Speaker and controller icons**: Clio, Nommo and Leviathan are detected as speakers. Wolverine, Kishi and Raiju are detected as controllers.

### Changed
- Unrecognized devices no longer fill an empty headphones/keyboard/mouse slot with the wrong icon. They get their own card with a generic battery icon. A second device of the same type also gets its own card now, instead of being dropped.

### Install
Download and run `Razer Battery Widget Setup 1.6.0.exe` below.
